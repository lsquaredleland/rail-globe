# Map QA: Western Europe

Date 2026-10-16. Scope: United Kingdom, Ireland, France, Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg, Germany, Switzerland, Austria, Spain, Portugal, Italy, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland (Iceland has no rail in the world build). Data: the world build in `app/public/data/world/` and `data/world/<cc>/` as found on 2026-10-16 (planet-260928, binary built 12:12). Nothing was committed; no world build was run.

## Method

- Dev server `http://localhost:5192` (already running on that port, same `app/`), driven by headless Chrome through `puppeteer-core` (script and PNGs in the session scratchpad, not in the repo). The shared claude-in-chrome browser was not used.
- 9 country views (z4.3 to z5.8) and 34 city or hub views (z9.3 to z13.2) in the three colour modes (Rail type, Operator colours, Speed), English labels (`lg=en`). The 9 country views and 26 of the 34 city and hub views were opened and looked at (Marseille, Rotterdam, Frankfurt, Stuttgart, Glasgow, Milan z10.5, Brussels z12.8 and Munich z13 were shot but only measured from the data, not looked at). London, Paris, Berlin, Brussels, Hamburg, Zurich, Madrid, Rome, Munich, Dublin, Stockholm, Amsterdam, Milan, Berlin Hbf, King's Cross and Zurich HB were studied in detail.
- Numbers were measured from `data/world/<cc>/{features.typed,stations,complexes,lines}` and `app/public/data/world/<cc>/stats.json`, against Wikipedia (metro, tram and high-speed lists, busiest stations), operator pages and OSM itself.
- Fixes were tested on a scratch copy only: `rail-extract cut --regions data/regions --data <scratch>/fix <slugs>` then `build <slugs> --no-tiles --data <scratch>/fix --out <scratch>/out`. A cut with the unchanged configs reproduced `data/world/<cc>/features.typed.geojsonl` byte for byte for ie, gb, de, it, es, at, ch (`cmp`), so the before and after numbers below are comparable. `app/public/data/world/` and `data/world/` were not touched: the fixes reach the map at the next world build.
