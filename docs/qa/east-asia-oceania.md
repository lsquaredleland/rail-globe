# Map QA: East Asia, Southeast Asia, Oceania (2026-10-16)

Scope: China (with Hong Kong, Taiwan), Japan, South Korea, Mongolia, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Philippines, Australia, New Zealand. World build as it stood on 2026-10-16 (`app/public/data/world/world.pmtiles`, `data/world/<slug>/`).

## Method

- Dev server on port 5194, headless Chrome (puppeteer-core, own profile, hash links `#r=world&c=<type|speed|operator>&v=z/lat/lon`). 15 country shots (z3.6 to z7.2) and 24 city shots (z9.6 to z10.8: Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen/Hong Kong, Guangzhou, Tokyo, Osaka, Seoul, Taipei, Bangkok, Jakarta, Sydney, Melbourne, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Manila, Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, Auckland, Wellington, Brisbane, Perth, Ulaanbaatar), colour modes type, speed and operator, HSR only. Every PNG was looked at except Auckland, Brisbane and Beijing-speed (not opened). Shots are in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.
- Data checks on `data/world/<slug>/` (typed track, `stations.geojsonl`, `lines.json`, `classify_report.json`) against Wikipedia and OSM tags. Heavy queries were not needed (the cut output holds the tags).
- Fixes: config only (`pipeline/config/*.json`), verified with `rail-extract cut` and `rail-extract build <slug> --no-tiles` (side files written to a scratch folder, not to `app/public`). **No world build, no tile rebuild**: `world.pmtiles` and the app's side files still show the old data until the next world build. `data/world/<slug>/` for au, nz, th, cn, jp, tw, id was rewritten by the cut/build.

Stale-exemption check (`check.py --exemptions`) passes for the two new exemptions (`airport-rail-link-not-high-speed` matched 17 ways, `wairarapa-connection` 92 ways).
